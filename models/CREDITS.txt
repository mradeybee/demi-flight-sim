Boeing 747 is an original high-detail procedural mesh in Turbo Flight Sim 0.1
(upper-deck hump, four high-bypass turbofans, wing fairings, dual window belts).
It is intentionally not the A380 GLB.

Models were scaled and reoriented for this game. Interactive opening parts
(doors, gear, flaps, and so on) and Demi Airline paint are original
Turbo Flight Sim 0.1 work.
