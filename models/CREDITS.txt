Collada sources converted to glTF 2.0:
- a318, a319, a321, a220 (CS300), a330, a340, beluga
- b747 (744), b757, b767, b777
- atr42, q400, crj700, crj900, e170, e190, bae146
- citation, pa28, ask21, heli (EC135)

Models were scaled and reoriented for this game. Interactive opening parts
(doors, gear, flaps, and so on) and Demi Airline paint are original
Turbo Flight Sim 0.1 work.
